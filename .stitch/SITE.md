# Penny Juice — Site Constitution
**Stitch Project ID:** [To be populated]

## 1. Core Identity
- **Project Name:** Penny Juice
- **Mission:** Provide organic, plant-based juices that refresh, nourish, heal and energize the body, offering high-quality nutritious drinks benefiting both health and the planet.
- **Target Audience:** Health-conscious consumers, wellness enthusiasts, families seeking natural beverages, ages 20-45
- **Voice:** Warm, energetic, trustworthy, nature-forward, premium yet accessible

## 2. Visual Language
- **Primary Vibe:** Fresh & Vibrant (sun-kissed, natural, alive)
- **Secondary Vibe:** Clean & Premium (minimal clutter, high-quality feel)
- **Tertiary Vibe:** Warm & Welcoming (inviting, friendly, community-oriented)
