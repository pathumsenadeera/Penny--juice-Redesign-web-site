# Design System: Penny Juice — Modern Redesign
**Project ID:** [To be populated after project creation]

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Penny Juice radiates a fresh, vibrant, and premium wellness brand feel. The design is clean and airy, with generous whitespace. The atmosphere is optimistic and energizing — like stepping into a sun-drenched farmer's market.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Penny Juice Orange** (#F9A602): Primary brand color; CTA buttons, active nav links, highlights
- **Fresh Harvest Green** (#7CB342): Secondary accent; badges, tags, success states
- **Cloud White Background** (#F8FAFC): Main page background
- **Pure White Cards** (#FFFFFF): Card backgrounds
- **Deep Charcoal Text** (#1A1A2E): Primary headings and body text
- **Soft Slate Gray** (#64748B): Secondary text, captions
- **Border Mist** (#E2E8F0): Dividers, input borders

## 3. Typography Rules
- Font Family: Poppins (Google Fonts), fallback Inter sans-serif
- Headings: Poppins SemiBold (600) or Bold (700)
- Body: Poppins Regular (400) at 16px, line-height 1.7
- Buttons: Poppins SemiBold (600)

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons (Primary):** Pill-shaped (border-radius 50px), orange fill (#F9A602), white text, hover lift
- **Buttons (Secondary):** Pill-shaped, transparent with orange border, fills on hover
- **Cards:** White, 16px border-radius, shadow 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.08), hover lift
- **Navigation:** Sticky top, white bg, 70px height
- **Input Fields:** 12px border-radius, white, 1.5px border #E2E8F0

## 5. Layout Principles
- Sticky nav: 70px height, Logo left | Nav center | Icons right
- Max-width: 1280px centered
- Grid: 12-column; Products 4-col desktop, 2-col tablet, 1-col mobile
- Section padding: 80px vertical on desktop
- Footer: #1A1A2E background, white text, 4-column layout
